# Migrating from 1.0.0-alpha.320

## A delayed call begins a chain of its own with `unchained`

The option of `context.delay` that makes the call by a chain of its own is `unchained`, where it was
`detached`. A call that still passes `detached: true` continues the chain that armed it, so an
operation re-arming itself with it is refused on its third round:

```javascript
await context.delay(endpoint, request, { interval, overdue: null, unchained: true })
```

An operation meant to be re-entered may now say so in its declaration instead, and then every call
to it begins a chain of its own. See [call cycles](/documentation/cycles.md#an-operation-meant-to-be-re-entered).

## A transition over `entries` writes all or nothing

A transition over `entries` whose set has one member changed under it by another call writes
nothing of the set, and is retried or raises `StateConcurrencyException`, as its `concurrency`
says. Before, the changed member was skipped, the rest was written, and the transition answered as
if all of it had been — with an event for every member, the skipped one included.

## What to do

1. Rename `detached` to `unchained` in every `context.delay` call.
